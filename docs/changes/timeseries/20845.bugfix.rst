``LombScargle`` with ``method="fastchi2"`` now computes ill-conditioned
frequencies from the weighted design matrix, avoiding inaccurate or negative
powers and singular-matrix errors for clustered observations and aliased
harmonics. Well-conditioned frequencies use batched normal-equation solves
with bounded temporary storage.

The ``chi2``, ``slow``, and ``cython`` Lomb-Scargle methods now compute the
least-squares projection at frequencies with a rank-deficient real sampled model,
instead of returning inconsistent powers or raising ``LinAlgError``.
``LombScargle.model`` and ``model_parameters`` use the corresponding
minimum-norm least-squares solution.
